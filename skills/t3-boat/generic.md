## Workers and briefs

No playbook is attached, so you decide what to delegate, to how many workers and in what order. Start every worker with `boat-worker start`.

1. Write one self-contained brief per worker: the goal, the repository URL, the scope, what is already known, constraints, the deliverable and how success is checked. Agree the deliverable and its form with the owner when it is not obvious, for example a report with file and line evidence or source links and dates, or code changes delivered the way the owner asks.
2. Any task fits a worker: inspection, implementation, tests, research or a review of a given commit. Choose the model for each brief from what `orchestrator_capabilities` lists, and name it and why in the parent update.
3. A worker may split its own work further when the brief allows it and its tools support it; ask it to name in its result what it handed on and what came back.
